WHO WE ARE

Bringing AI into Academic Evaluation

ScriptVision is an AI-powered handwritten answer evaluation system designed to support teachers and students in the assessment process. It combines handwritten text recognition and semantic similarity analysis to help evaluate student answers against model answers.

Our goal is to reduce repetitive evaluation work, support consistent assessment, and provide students with meaningful feedback on their academic performance.

◎

Our Mission

To make answer evaluation more efficient and informative by using AI to assist teachers and help students understand their strengths and areas for improvement.

◇

Our Vision

To support a smarter academic environment where technology assists educators and helps learners make better progress through useful evaluation feedback.

✧

Our Approach

We combine OCR, natural language processing, and semantic similarity techniques to assist in evaluating handwritten answers while keeping teachers involved in the assessment process.

WHAT WE DO

Designed for Better Evaluation

Technology that supports the evaluation process from submission to feedback.

01

Handwritten Text Recognition

Extracts text from uploaded handwritten answer scripts using optical character recognition.

02

AI-Assisted Evaluation

Compares extracted answers with model answers using semantic similarity techniques.

03

Teacher Review

Allows teachers to review AI-assisted evaluation results and make adjustments where necessary.

04

Meaningful Feedback

Helps students understand their marks, identify missing points, and recognize areas for improvement.

Building a Smarter Evaluation Experience
