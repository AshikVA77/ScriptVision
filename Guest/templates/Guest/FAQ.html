ScriptVision FAQ

Find quick answers to common questions.

01 What is ScriptVision? +
ScriptVision is an AI-based handwritten answer evaluation system that extracts handwritten answers, evaluates them, generates marks, and provides feedback.
02 Who can use ScriptVision? +
The system is designed for teachers and students in educational institutions.
03 How are answer sheets evaluated? +
The teacher uploads answer sheets, and the AI processes the handwriting, compares it with the model answer, and generates marks and feedback.
04 Can the AI read handwritten answers? +
Yes. ScriptVision uses OCR and AI models to recognize and evaluate handwritten text.
05 Can teachers modify the marks? +
Yes. Teachers can review AI-generated results and make changes if required.
06 Can students view feedback? +
Yes. Students can log in and view marks, strengths, missing points, and feedback.
07 Does ScriptVision support diagrams? +
Yes. The system is designed to analyze diagrams and handwritten figures along with textual answers.
08 Is the uploaded data secure? +
Uploaded answer sheets and evaluation reports are intended to be securely stored and accessible only to authorized users.
09 Can teachers evaluate multiple answer sheets? +
Yes. Teachers can upload and evaluate multiple student answer sheets.
10 Who developed ScriptVision? +
ScriptVision is an academic project developed for AI-based handwritten answer evaluation.
